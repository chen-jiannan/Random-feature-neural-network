# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and documentation, not people
- Use welcoming and constructive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and levels of experience

## Unacceptable behavior

- Personal attacks
- Harassment or discrimination
- Deliberate intimidation
- Publishing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove or reject comments, issues, or contributions that violate these standards.
